Get started

01

Connect

Plug the network cable into your wall socket and the power adapter into the back of HUSH. The screen lights up in about a minute.

02

Join HUSH Wi-Fi

Scan the code on the screen with your phone to join HUSH Wi-Fi and download the HUSH app.

Mobile data and other networks are not covered.

03

Choose a plan

Private Hours are only available with an active plan. Choose one in the app and confirm payment. Until then, the screen shows Choose a plan to start and the start button stays locked.

HUSH HOME04
One home, one timer

Sharing with your household

Private Hours belong to the whole home. While protection is on, every device on HUSH Wi-Fi is covered and the household balance counts down — whether one person is using it or five.

A

Pause requests

If you tap Pause while other members are still active, they receive a request: "Someone at home wants to pause Private Protection." If nobody answers within 30 seconds, the home pauses.

B

Keep me private

Any active member can tap Keep me private. Protection stays on for the whole home and the timer keeps running for everyone.

C

Step out

Done, but don't want to stop anyone else? Tap Step out in the app. Your device leaves the session and is no longer protected. The household timer keeps running.

D

Member limits

The account holder can set a monthly limit for each member in Settings › Household, so one person can't use the family's hours.

Household tip

Agree on a routine. A session someone else keeps open still counts against everyone's hours.

When things don't go as planned

When your hours run out

00:00
  • The status light turns coral: Private Protection Unavailable.
  • Wi-Fi keeps working. Nothing switches off.
  • Each service returns to its normal tracking and sharing settings.
  • Add 1 Private Hour for $3.99, change plan, or wait for renewal.

Troubleshooting

The start button is greyed out.
Your household has no active plan, or your last payment didn't go through. Check Plans & Billing in the app.
My phone says "Hush Disconnected" but the screen says protection is active.
Your phone is on mobile data or another network, or you tapped Step out. HUSH only covers devices in the current session on HUSH Wi-Fi.
My phone and the router show different hours remaining.
Displays sync every few minutes. The router screen shows your household's official balance.
I paused, but the timer is still running.
Another member tapped Keep me private. Use Step out to leave the session yourself.
I paused HUSH for a quick search, and now things at work feel different.
Activity while paused is shared under each service's normal settings, including derived signals passed to participating employment services. HUSH cannot recall them later. Keep a session active whenever you're job-hunting.
A website still knew who I was during a session.
Information you choose to send — logins, bookings, payments, messages — still reaches that service. Protection is not anonymity.

The small print

Six questions people ask in the first week, answered plainly.

Per month, billed in advance, renews automatically. Unused hours expire at renewal. Extra hour $3.99.

How is an hour counted?
Once for the whole home.

Eleven devices on HUSH Wi-Fi still use one hour, not eleven. The clock runs while anyone keeps protection on.

What happens while I'm paused?
Tracking is back on.

What's collected while paused may be shared and scored later, even after you resume. Inferred signals ("derived data") can pass between participating services.

Does buying time erase the past?
No.

Starting a session, adding hours or changing plans never removes data already collected or profiles that already exist.

Does HUSH listen to my home?
No.

It doesn't hear the room or read your activity. It does keep the start and end time of every session, for billing and service improvement.

Who is covered?
Participating services.

The list lives at hush.home/participating and may change monthly. Emergency services can access your connection at any time.

Pause requests expire after 30 seconds. A member who steps out is not protected for the rest of that session. Protection covers new tracking, profiling and identifiable data sharing on HUSH Wi-Fi during an active session. Protection is not anonymity.
