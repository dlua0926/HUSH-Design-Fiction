PROCESS · 202611 · Generative AI usage record

Our project, with AI as a tool.

HUSH is our group's idea, world and story. We used AI for two things: generating images (product renders, concept sketches, guide illustrations) and polishing copy (turning our notes and decisions into clean English, and setting it on the page). Every decision about the world is ours: the what-if, the scenario, the plans, prices, hours, household rules and fine print. The physical model and the Figma prototypes are our own work.

Tools: Codex (OpenAI) for image generation, Claude (Anthropic) for copy and layout, ChatGPT (OpenAI) for one review of the draft. Appendix A gives the image prompts in full (P1–P18). Appendix B gives the instructions we gave the AI, verbatim (T1–T13). Full chat transcripts are kept by Jinge and can be provided on request.
