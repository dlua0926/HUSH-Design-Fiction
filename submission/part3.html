11 · Generative AI usage record

How AI was used, and what we decided.

AI was used as a sketching, drafting and image-production tool. The team made every decision about the world: the what-if, the scenario, the plans, prices, hours, household rules and fine print. The physical model and its photographs are our own work. All text that appears inside AI-generated images was checked against the world rules, and images were regenerated until the wording was correct. The full prompts follow (P1–P14).
