## Gap handling and construction advice
Centre actual iPad in aperture; measure top and bottom gaps individually. If truly centred, each gap is (aperture height minus iPad outer height)/2. Make white thick-card inner strips, optionally stiffened by foamboard. Attach to frame, not display; do not cover active touch area. Remove front glass/clear panel for direct interaction.

Use rigid easel/stand/base to carry iPad weight; clay is decorative, not structural. Antennas can wrap a paper-straw core and mount on rear frame. Retain charge access, ventilation and removability. Test with actual materials before final assembly.
