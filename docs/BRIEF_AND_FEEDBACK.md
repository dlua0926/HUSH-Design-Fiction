## Assignment requirements (summary)

Work from faint signals to what-if and familiar archetype. Show the world through in-world details and allow audience interpretation.

1. High-fidelity diegetic artefact(s).
2. Instruction set, such as packaging directions or a quick-start guide; malfunction scenarios may add depth.
3. Advertisement showing how people in the imagined world would encounter the product. Permitted examples include unboxing video, short social ad, magazine/catalogue insert, outdoor ad and landing page. A video is NOT mandatory.
4. Presentation slides.
Compile materials as a PDF for Canvas; photograph physical models. Keep records of generative-AI usage, including prompts. The supplied snapshot shows a due date of 8 November 2026, 11:59 pm; verify live Canvas for final schedule rather than treating this historical snapshot as current authority.
